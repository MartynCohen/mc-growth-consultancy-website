Privacy Notice

What I collect, why, who sees it and how to get it changed or deleted. Written to be read.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who I am

MC Growth Consultancy is the trading name of Martyn Cohen, a sole trader based in England. I'm the data controller for the personal information described here. If you have any question about it, email {{email}} or call {{phone}}.

2. What I collect

Only what you choose to give me, plus anonymous visitor statistics if you agree to them.

  • Enquiries and call bookings. Your name, contact details, company and whatever you tell me in your message or on the call.
  • Job applications and CVs. Your name, contact details, town, CV and any message you include.
  • Job adverts. The employer's contact details and the details of the role.
  • Buyer registrations and seller enquiries. Your contact details and what you tell me about what you're looking to buy or sell.
  • Client work. Information you share with me in the course of a recruitment search, a business review or a sale.

Please don't send sensitive personal information, such as health details, through the website forms.

3. Why I use it, and the legal basis

  • To reply to you and provide the service you've asked about. This is in my legitimate interests and, where we go on to work together, necessary for our contract.
  • To pass a job application to the employer. Only with your consent, which you give when you apply.
  • To keep your CV on file for future roles. Only if you tick the box asking me to.
  • To tell registered buyers about businesses for sale. Only because you asked me to when you registered.
  • To see which pages of the site are useful. Only with your consent to analytics cookies.
  • To keep business records. Where the law requires it, for example for tax.

I don't sell personal information and I don't use it for automated decision-making.

4. Who sees it

  • Employers. If you apply for a job, your application goes to me first and then to the employer advertising that role. I don't send your CV to anyone else without asking you.
  • Buyers and sellers. Details of a business for sale are only released to a buyer after a non-disclosure agreement is signed. A buyer's details are only passed to a seller with the buyer's agreement.
  • Service providers. The website and its forms are hosted by Netlify. Call bookings are handled by Calendly. Visitor statistics, if you consent, are provided by Google Analytics. Email and documents are held in standard business software. Some of these providers store data outside the UK and rely on recognised safeguards to do so.
  • Professional advisers and authorities. Where needed, for example my accountant, or where the law requires it.

5. How long I keep it

  • General enquiries: up to two years after our last contact.
  • Job applications: up to six months after the role closes, or up to two years if you've asked me to keep your CV on file.
  • Buyer registrations: until you ask me to remove you, and reviewed every two years.
  • Client records: six years after the work ends, to meet tax and legal requirements.

You can ask me to delete your information sooner at any time.

6. Cookies

The site works without cookies. If you say yes to the cookie question, Google Analytics sets cookies so I can see anonymous figures such as which pages are visited most. If you say no, it never loads. Your choice is remembered in your browser, and you can change it at any time using the Cookie choices link at the bottom of every page.

The booking diary on the contact page is provided by Calendly and only loads when you ask to see available times. Calendly may set its own cookies when it does.

7. Your rights

You can ask me to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, stop or limit how I use it, or send it to someone else. Where I rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. Email {{email}} and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Security

I take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including using reputable providers and limiting access to me. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

If this notice changes I'll update this page and the date at the top.
