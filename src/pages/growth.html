Where I Usually Start: a Business Review

Before changing anything I want to see how the business really runs, not how it looks on paper. That means time on site: sitting in the showroom or the contracts office, following an order from enquiry to fitting, and talking to each person in the team.

Then I go through the numbers with you. Where the margin is made, where it leaks, which customers and products earn their keep and which don't.

You get a written report with clear priorities and a plan in an order you can actually work through. Then we decide together how much of it you want my help with.
