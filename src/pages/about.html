About

Hello, I'm Martyn

I set up MC Growth Consultancy to give flooring business owners the kind of straight, experienced help I'd have wanted on my side of the desk. It's just me, and flooring is all I do.

The Short Version

I've spent more than 25 years in retail and commercial leadership. My career has taken in Halifax, Phones4u, Allied Carpets, Dreams, EE, Virgin Media and Safestore, then Carpetright, where I was Head of Operations and In-Home Services, and Headlam Group, where I was Group Sales Director.

Along the way I've led large sales and operations teams, run in-home selling and mobile showrooms, overseen a network of more than 1,300 self-employed fitters, brought new business systems into a national retailer and led a sales transformation across a group of 32 businesses.

In 2025 I started MC Growth Consultancy, and I've worked only with flooring businesses since: independent retailers, contract flooring companies, distributors and suppliers. Some need the right person hiring. Some want to grow without everything running through the owner. Some are thinking about selling. Quite often it's all three, in that order.
