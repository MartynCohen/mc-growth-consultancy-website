Trade shows and exhibitions remain some of the most powerful opportunities for suppliers, retailers, and industry professionals in the home furnishings and flooring sectors. These events are not just about showcasing products - they are about building relationships, discovering trends, and driving business growth.

The Power of Exhibiting: Why Suppliers Should Get Involved

Exhibiting at trade shows offers a unique set of benefits:

  • Direct Access to Decision Makers: Meet thousands of retailers, contractors, designers, architects, and specifiers in one place, all looking for the latest innovations and partnerships.
  • Showcase New Products: Launch and demonstrate new ranges, materials, and technology to a highly engaged audience.
  • Build Brand Awareness: Stand out in a crowded market by putting your brand in front of key buyers and influencers.
  • Network and Collaborate: Forge new relationships with industry peers, potential clients, and collaborators.
  • Stay Ahead of Trends: See first-hand what's new, what's next, and how your business can adapt to changing market demands.
  • Gather Market Intelligence: Learn from seminars, workshops, and competitor displays to inform your own strategy.

Who Should Attend?

Suppliers and Manufacturers

Looking to expand market reach, launch products, or connect with retailers and distributors.

Retailers and Contractors

Seeking new products, inspiration, and supplier relationships.

Designers and Architects

Exploring the latest trends, materials, and solutions for client projects.

Installers and Fitters

Engaging with new tools, techniques, and training opportunities.
