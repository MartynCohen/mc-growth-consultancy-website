Home improvement retail is experiencing a major shift as traditional business models evolve to meet modern consumer demands. One of the most significant trends in 2025 is the emergence of small format retail stores.

These compact outlets blend convenience with specialisation and are strategically located in urban and suburban areas to provide quick, easy access to key home improvement products.

Why Small Format Stores Matter

Convenience

Customers can shop for essential supplies without travelling to large out of town superstores.

Personalised Service

Smaller locations often emphasise expert advice and project support, creating a more intimate and value driven shopping experience.

Curated Selection

By focusing inventory on high demand items and new product innovations, small format stores reduce overwhelm and streamline the purchasing process.

Digital Integration: The Game Changer

Equally important is the increasing digital integration within the sector:

Seamless Online to Offline Journeys

Retailers offer click and collect, online inventories and flexible delivery options, empowering shoppers to plan and purchase exactly how they want.

Mobile Tools and Apps

From virtual design consultations to augmented reality visualisers, digital tools are helping customers visualise outcomes and make informed choices.

Real Time Expert Chat

Tech savvy customers expect on demand support, whether that is scheduling a video consult with a flooring expert or receiving tailored DIY tutorials.

The Winning Formula

Retailers that successfully combine small format locations with robust digital platforms are well positioned to attract busy, information hungry homeowners who value both convenience and expertise.
