For owners

Thinking of selling? Start with a conversation.

Most owners sell a business once. There's no practice run, and the things that cost people money are rarely the things they were worried about.

  1. A confidential conversation20 to 30 minutes, by phone or in person. No cost, no obligation and nothing goes anywhere.
  2. Assessment and valuationWe go through your accounts, spend time on site and look at the business the way a buyer will. You get a written valuation with the reasoning shown and a ranked list of what's holding the value down. Fixed fee, agreed upfront.
  3. The saleAn anonymised sale pack, buyer approaches, NDAs, negotiation and completion. Your job is to keep running the business. Ours is everything else.

Not selling this year? Two or three years out is often the better time to talk.

How we protect a sale

Confidential from the first call

  • Anonymous until NDA

    No business is identifiable in anything we publish or send until a buyer has signed a non-disclosure agreement.

  • Your sign-off, every time

    Nothing goes to market, and nothing goes to a buyer, without the owner's approval.

  • No direct approaches

    Buyers agree not to contact staff, customers or suppliers. Every enquiry comes through us.

  • Honest about timescales

    Six to twelve months from going to market to completion is normal. Anyone promising eight weeks is selling you something.
