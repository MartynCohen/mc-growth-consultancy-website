Common Questions

Do you only work with flooring businesses?

Yes. Retailers, contract flooring companies, distributors, manufacturers and suppliers to the trade. I've worked only in flooring since I set up MC Growth Consultancy, and that's deliberate. It means I already understand your suppliers, your margins and your fitters before we start.

How do you charge?

It depends on the work, and you'll always know before anything starts. Recruitment is a fee on a successful placement. A business sale is a fixed assessment fee followed by a percentage when the sale completes. Consultancy is a day rate or a fixed project price. The first conversation is always free.

How long does a piece of work take?

Anything from a single day to an arrangement that runs for several months. A business sale takes longer: six to twelve months from going to market is normal for a deal of any substance. I'll give you a realistic timescale at the start rather than an optimistic one.

Will I be dealing with you personally?

Yes. I take the brief, do the work and stay in touch. If I ever need to bring in a specialist, such as a solicitor or an accountant, I'll tell you first and you'll know exactly who they are.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. I'm based in Hertfordshire and work with flooring businesses across the UK. Reviews, recruitment briefs and anything involving your team work best face to face. Follow-ups are often quicker on a call.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. That matters most when you're thinking about selling or replacing someone, so nothing is shared without your say-so and I'm happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before we get into detail.
