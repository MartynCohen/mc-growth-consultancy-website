Where I Usually Start: a Business Review

Before changing anything I want to see how the business really runs, not how it looks on paper. That means time on site: sitting in the showroom or the contracts office, following an order from enquiry to fitting, and talking to each person in the team.

Then I go through the numbers with you. Where the margin is made, where it leaks, which customers and products earn their keep and which don't.

You get a written report with clear priorities and a plan in an order you can actually work through. Then we decide together how much of it you want my help with.

What I Work On

Usually two or three of these at once, because they're connected.

Sales development

How enquiries are handled, how quotes are followed up, conversion and average order value. In the showroom, on contract work and in the customer's home.

  • Lead handling and quote follow-up
  • Selling on value, not price
  • In-home and mobile showroom selling

Performance management

Clear targets, a small number of measures that matter and regular conversations about them, so good people know where they stand and poor performance gets dealt with.

  • KPIs your team understand
  • One-to-ones that mean something
  • Coaching for your managers

Customer experience

The journey from first enquiry to aftercare, written down and followed every time. It's what gets you recommended, and what an online retailer can't copy.

  • Customer journey and service standards
  • Complaints handled properly
  • Reviews and referrals

Buying and suppliers

The right range, bought on the right terms. I've sat on both sides of that table, so I know where the room to negotiate usually is.

  • Terms, rebates and exclusivity

New stores and expansion

Opening a second site, adding a contract division or taking on franchise partners. Worth planning properly before you sign a lease.

  • Finding franchise partners

Interim leadership

When you need an experienced pair of hands for a few months: covering a senior gap, leading a change, or running a project nobody has time for.
