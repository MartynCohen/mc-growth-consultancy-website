From Corporate To Consultancy: Martyn Cohen on The UK Flooring Podcast

29 June 2025 · 2 min read · Martyn Cohen

We are excited to share Martyn Cohen's appearance on the UK Flooring Podcast, Episode 7: "From Corporate to Consultancy." He joins host Tom Cockerill for an open conversation about career reinvention, running high-performing flooring teams, and how to scale a business without losing your sanity.

Career Reinvention in the Flooring Industry

Martyn's journey from corporate leadership to founding MC Growth Consultancy offers valuable insights for anyone considering a career pivot within the flooring sector. His experience demonstrates how industry expertise can be leveraged to create new opportunities and serve the sector in different ways.

Building High-Performance Teams

During the conversation, Martyn shares practical strategies for developing teams that consistently deliver exceptional results. From recruitment and training to performance management and retention, the discussion covers the essential elements of team building in the flooring industry.

Scaling Without Sacrificing Sanity

One of the key topics explored is how flooring business owners can grow their operations while maintaining work-life balance and preserving their mental well-being. Martyn discusses the systems, processes, and mindset shifts needed to achieve sustainable growth.

About The UK Flooring Podcast

Hosted by Tom Cockerill of Cockerill & Co, The UK Flooring Podcast brings together industry leaders, entrepreneurs, and experts to share their stories and insights. Each episode offers practical advice and inspiration for flooring professionals looking to grow their businesses and advance their careers.

Connect with MC Growth Consultancy

If Martyn's insights on the podcast resonated with you, MC Growth Consultancy offers specialised support for flooring businesses. From team development and performance coaching to strategic planning and growth acceleration, we help flooring companies achieve their full potential.

The flooring industry continues to evolve, and conversations like these help professionals stay ahead of the curve. We thank Tom Cockerill and The UK Flooring Podcast for providing a platform for these important discussions.
