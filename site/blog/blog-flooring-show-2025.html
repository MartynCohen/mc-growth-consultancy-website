Make the Most of The Flooring Show 2025: Your Essential Guide

14 September 2025 · 2 min read · Martyn Cohen

The Flooring Show 2025 promises to be the year's most important gathering for flooring professionals. Whether you're a retailer, contractor, or supplier, this comprehensive guide will help you maximise every moment at Harrogate.

What to Expect This Year

The 2025 show brings together the latest innovations in LVT, carpet trends, and sustainable flooring solutions. Key exhibitors will showcase cutting-edge products that are reshaping both commercial and residential markets across the UK.

Spotlight: The UK Flooring Podcast & Cockerill & Co

A real highlight this year is The UK Flooring Podcast, recorded live from Hall Q. Tom and Sarah Cockerill of Cockerill & Co will host sessions across the show, sharing powerful stories and practical advice for flooring professionals. They offer lessons from building and turning around flooring businesses, helping retailers and contractors tackle current growth challenges.

Key Networking Opportunities

  • Early morning supplier briefings with industry leaders
  • The UK Flooring Podcast live every day, offering fresh perspectives and expert interviews
  • Evening networking events connecting retailers, contractors, and suppliers
  • Innovation showcases featuring sustainable and smart flooring solutions
  • Regional business roundtables for targeted market discussions

Making the Most of Your Visit

Plan your route in advance to ensure you visit key suppliers and attend priority presentations. Book meetings early with major exhibitors, and don't miss the opportunity to connect with fellow professionals who understand the unique challenges of the UK flooring market.

The Flooring Show 2025 represents more than just an exhibition - it's where the future of our industry takes shape. From breakthrough products to business-changing partnerships, Harrogate offers the platform for flooring professionals to grow, learn, and succeed.

Connect With MC Growth Consultancy

MC Growth Consultancy will be attending The Flooring Show 2025. Contact us to arrange a meeting and discover how our expertise in flooring business growth, recruitment strategies, and performance management can help take your business to the next level.
