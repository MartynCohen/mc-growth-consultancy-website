Coaching for Success: Unlocking People Potential in the Flooring Industry

26 August 2025 · 3 min read · Martyn Cohen

The People Advantage

In the flooring industry, success is rarely down to products alone. The real differentiator is people.

On-Site Excellence

A fitter going the extra mile in a customer's home

Sales Mastery

A store team who can turn browsers into loyal clients

Your people are your business.

The Overlooked Opportunity

And yet, too many flooring companies overlook one of the most powerful tools for growth: coaching. Done well, coaching does not just improve skills, it builds confidence, resilience and motivation.

In short, it turns a workforce into a winning team.

Why Ongoing Development is Non Negotiable

Not so long ago, flooring was seen as a job for life. Fitters and sales staff would stay with one company for decades. But today's workforce is different. People move on more quickly, and the best talent is always in demand.

This makes continuous development essential. By investing in people, whether they are apprentices, seasoned fitters or store managers, you give them:

  • The confidence to make decisions in the moment
  • The skills to adapt when markets shift
  • The feeling that they matter, and that their future is worth investing in

And when people feel valued, they do not just stay. They perform.

Coaching: More Than Training

Training shows people what to do. Coaching shows them how to think.

A great coach helps employees find their own solutions, own their actions and take pride in results. That is a game changer in flooring, where fitters and sales teams face unique challenges every day.

When people are coached to think for themselves, you create:

  • Teams who are faster to embrace change
  • Individuals who feel empowered rather than directed
  • A culture where ideas and initiative thrive

Using Simple Coaching Models

Many managers hesitate because coaching feels vague. That is where models like GROW come in:

The GROW Model

Goal - What do you want to achieve?

Reality - What is happening now?

Options - What could you do?

Will - What will you do next?

This simple structure turns a chat into a coaching conversation, one that leaves the employee motivated and accountable.

Best Practice in Flooring Businesses

Here is how coaching can come alive on the shop floor or out in the field:

Buddying

Pair new staff with experienced colleagues so learning is fast and practical

Regular One to Ones

Not tick box reviews, but meaningful conversations that stretch and support

Strength Based Coaching

Help each person focus on what they do best, then build from there

Idea Sharing

Create forums for teams to bring forward suggestions, some of the best innovations come from the front line

The Impact You Can Expect

Companies that embrace coaching see measurable results:

  • Stronger sales and higher conversion rates
  • Lower staff turnover and better retention of skilled fitters
  • Smoother operations and fewer mistakes
  • Customers who notice the difference and return because of it

Key Insight

In a competitive market, these gains are not just nice to have. They are what keep you ahead.

Recommended Reading

If you would like to explore the power of coaching further, here are three excellent books:

Coaching for Performance by Sir John Whitmore

The definitive guide to the GROW model

The Coaching Habit by Michael Bungay Stanier

Practical and easy to use coaching questions for busy leaders

Drive by Daniel H Pink

A fascinating look at what really motivates people to perform at their best
