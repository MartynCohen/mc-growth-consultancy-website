Systems and AI

A Business That Runs Without You in Every Decision

If the way things get done lives in your head, the business can't grow past you, and it's worth less when you come to sell. I help you write it down, put the right tools around it and use AI where it saves real time.

What This Covers

Pick what the business needs now. It rarely needs all of it at once.

Processes and procedures

Enquiry, survey, quote, order, fitting and aftercare, written as short procedures a new starter could follow on day one.

  • Customer journey mapped end to end
  • Standard operating procedures
  • Checklists and templates

CRM and job management

Choosing the right system, setting it up around how you work and, the hard part, getting everyone to use it.

  • What to buy, and what not to
  • Set-up, pipelines and automation
  • Training and adoption

Numbers you can run the business on

A one-page view of the handful of figures that tell you how the week went, so decisions are based on facts and not on feel.

  • The right KPIs for your business
  • Weekly and monthly reporting
  • Margin by job, range and customer

AI in the day job

I've used AI tools every day for two years and now set them up for clients. Used well, they take hours out of the week. Used badly, they just produce more noise.

  • Quotes, follow-ups and customer emails
  • Job descriptions, procedures and reports
  • Meeting notes and actions

Installer frameworks

How you take on, brief, check and pay fitters. Clear standards protect your customers, and the right paperwork protects you.

  • Onboarding and standards
  • Quality checks and complaints
  • Agreements that reflect reality

Making change stick

Most new systems fail because of people, not software. I spend as much time with your team as I do on the system itself.

  • Team involved from the start
  • Hands-on training
  • Follow-up after go-live

What I've Worked With

I've led system change from the business side in large retailers and across a national group, so I know what these projects feel like for the people using them. I've worked with Salesforce, HubSpot, Monday.com, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and GoHighLevel, as well as bespoke systems.

I'll recommend what suits your business, not the most expensive option. If a spreadsheet and a shared calendar will do the job for now, that's what I'll tell you.

Why this matters if you plan to sell

A buyer pays more for a business that doesn't depend on its owner. Documented processes and reliable numbers are two of the cheapest ways to add value before a sale. More on selling your business.
